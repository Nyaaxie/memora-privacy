Memora Privacy Policy

Developer: jamesbrizuela
Last updated: October 28, 2026

About this policy

Memora is an offline personal journal provided by jamesbrizuela. This policy describes how the current offline version handles your information. You do not need an account to use Memora.

Information stored on your device

Memora stores the content you choose to enter, including diary titles and descriptions, photos, categories, moods and daily check-ins, tasks, due dates, reflections, and calendar events. App preferences, reminder settings, and camera-recovery drafts are also stored locally. This information supports your journal, calendar, monthly summaries, and reminders. Memora does not automatically send this content to the developer or to an AI or cloud service.

Permissions and app lock

Camera access is used when you choose to take a photo. The system photo and document pickers provide files you choose to attach or import. Notification permission is used for local daily and task reminders; Memora does not register remote push tokens. Optional app lock uses your device’s authentication system. Memora receives an authentication result and does not receive or store your fingerprint, face template, or device passcode. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Sharing, backups, and external services

Memora has no advertising, analytics, account, automatic cloud-sync, or AI service integration in this offline version. The developer does not sell your journal data. Exporting a backup creates an unencrypted JSON file containing your entries, attached photos, tasks, calendar events, and daily check-ins. When you choose a destination in the system share sheet or file picker, that app or storage provider may process or upload the file under its own policy. Memora does not control those destinations. Device or operating-system backup and restore may also copy app data according to your device settings; offline operation does not disable your device’s backup services.

Storage and security

Journal records are stored in an on-device SQLite database and app files. Memora does not add its own encryption to these records or exported backups. App lock controls access through the app and does not encrypt backup files. Your device’s security protections still apply. Keep your device secure and store exported copies privately. The developer cannot recover journal content that exists only on your device.

Retention and deleting your data

Your content remains on your device until you delete it or clear the app’s storage. You can delete diary entries, tasks, and calendar events within Memora. Deleted legacy records may leave hidden database metadata; deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure. To remove all local app data, use your device’s clear-storage option or uninstall Memora. Copies you exported, shared, or stored in device/cloud backups must be removed separately from those destinations. Device restore settings may restore previously backed-up app data. There is no Memora account or developer-hosted journal database to delete.

Contact and support

For privacy questions or support, contact jamesbrizuela at jamesbrizuela.17@gmail.com. If you email us, the developer and the email provider receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request and retained only as needed for support or applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email. Do not send your full journal or backup unless you intend to share it. Copying diagnostic details in Settings copies the app version, platform, and OS version to your clipboard; it does not automatically send a report.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Memora’s features or data practices change. The updated date will appear here and in the policy provided with the app.
